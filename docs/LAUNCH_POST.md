**Tweet 2 — How it works**

> How ChainChit works:
> 1️⃣ Friends form a group — fixed contribution, fixed cycles
> 2️⃣ Each cycle, members bid the lowest unique fee to win the pool
> 3️⃣ Lowest unique bid wins — competitive savings, fully on-chain
> 4️⃣ Reputation score (0–1000) tracks payment history — built-in trust layer
>
> 💡 Commit-reveal bidding prevents sniping. Every payout is a smart contract
> execution.
